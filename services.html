C-200  ·  Process

How an engagement runs

You know the price and the deliverable before any work begins. If scope changes, we re-price it in writing rather than letting the invoice drift.

Step 01

Call

Thirty minutes, free. What you are building, what is blocking it, and whether I am the right person for it.

Step 02

Proposal

A written scope with deliverables, milestones, timeline and fixed price. Nothing starts until you sign it.

Step 03

Build

Work happens against the milestones, with progress updates at each one. You see it as it develops.

Step 04

Handoff

Source files, schematics, documentation and a walkthrough. You own everything, and you can maintain it without me.

C-300  ·  Enquiry

Tell me what you are building

The more concrete you can be about the problem and the deadline, the faster I can tell you whether I am the right fit — including when I am not.
